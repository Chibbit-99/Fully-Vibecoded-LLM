How it works

  1. Retrieve. Your question is matched against a factbook of passages built from the training corpus (BM25, in your browser).
  2. Render. The passage and question become a chat prompt: <|user|>…<|context|>…<|assistant|>
  3. Generate. The transformer runs here, token by token, and stops when it emits <|end|>.

What it is not

Spore has no knowledge beyond a few megabytes of Wikipedia and children's stories, and no ability to reason. If it says something wrong, that is expected — every weight was trained from scratch on two CPU cores.
